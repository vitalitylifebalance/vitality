Fundamentals

What is Grounding?

Grounding (or earthing) is a simple technique: walking barefoot on earth, grass, or sand. The Earth is an inexhaustible source of free electrons. Published research suggests that, upon contact with the body, they may act as natural antioxidants and help neutralise free radicals.

Electron Transfer

Free electrons from the Earth flow into the body, helping neutralise pro-oxidant free radicals associated with chronic inflammation.

Circadian Rhythm

The geomagnetic field is thought to help regulate the cortisol-melatonin cycle, supporting the natural biological clock and sleep quality.

Nervous System

Several studies document activation of the parasympathetic nervous system —the body's "rest and recovery" mode— during contact with the Earth.

Systemic Inflammation

In the populations studied, researchers have measured decreases in pro-inflammatory markers such as IL-6, TNF-α, and CRP. Promising data that science continues to expand on.
