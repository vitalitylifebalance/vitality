Where Vitality was born

There was a before and an after. One day I could do anything — train, eat whatever I wanted, live without limits. The next, my body simply shut down. For 3 years I was trapped in an illness no one could explain: a chronic lung condition that stole my breath, my energy, and my freedom to live normally.

Doctor after doctor, test after test — and the same answer every time: "It's chronic. Learn to live with it."

But I refused to accept that sentence.

I immersed myself in research on bioenergetics, biophysics and bioelectricity. I discovered what scientists like Becker and Nordenström had already documented: that we are bioelectric beings, and that disconnection from the Earth has consequences for our natural balance.

That research changed my life and the way I understand the human body. And it gave me a mission: to make what I'd learned accessible, so no one has to walk that path alone. That's how Vitality Life Balance was born — not in a laboratory, but in the determination of someone who refused to give up.

We created Vipassanna: a biohacking device based on a galvanic interaction of biocompatible metals, generating biocompatible microvoltage on skin contact — with no batteries or external electricity. Our body is a bioelectric system, and Vipassanna is designed to create an environment that favours that natural balance. Together with our line of silver-conductive grounding textiles, we built Europe's first bioelectric wellness ecosystem.
