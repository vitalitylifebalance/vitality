Right of withdrawal: 14 days

You have 14 calendar days from receiving the product to return it without giving any reason, as established by law for distance purchases.

Return shipping costs are borne by the customer. The product must be returned complete (cables, adapters and testers included) and in perfect condition. Once the product is received and checked, we refund you within a maximum of 14 days via the same payment method you used.

03 — Warranty

3-year legal warranty

All our products carry the 3-year legal conformity warranty established by Spanish law for new products.

What it covers

Manufacturing and functional defects — for example, the conductive fabric no longer conducting under normal use and care. And here's the advantage of our system: you don't have to trust a feeling — run the included tester over the fabric and see whether it conducts. That objective check is the basis of any warranty claim.

What it doesn't cover

Normal wear, damage caused by use or washing contrary to the instructions, and the absence of subjective wellness sensations. We say it across the site and repeat it here: individual results vary and "not feeling anything" is not a product defect. What we guarantee is what can be measured: the product conducts and the circuit closes.

How to claim

Email vitalitylifebalance@gmail.com with your order number, a description of the issue and, for conduction issues, a photo or video of the tester result. Depending on the case: repair, replacement, price reduction or contract termination, as provided by law.
