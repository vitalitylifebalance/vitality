03 — Cookies

Cookie Policy

ePrivacy Directive · GDPR

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser or on your device when you visit a website. They are used to make the website work properly, to improve your browsing experience, to analyse traffic, and to show you relevant advertising.

Types of cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies

These are essential for the website to function. Without them, the site cannot provide essential services such as the shopping cart, the checkout process, or access to secure areas. They do not require your consent.

  • _session_id: manages the user's session
  • cart: stores the shopping cart contents (Shopify)
  • secure_customer_sig: customer authentication (Shopify)

Analytics cookies

These allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. All information these cookies collect is aggregated and therefore anonymous.

  • _ga, _gid, _gat: Google Analytics — analysis of user behaviour

Marketing cookies

These are used to track visitors across websites. The intention is to display ads that are relevant and engaging for the individual user.

  • _fbp: Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — advertising conversion tracking
  • ttclid: TikTok — campaign tracking

Managing cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish. You can delete all cookies already on your device, and you can set most browsers to prevent them from being placed. However, if you do this, you may have to manually adjust some preferences every time you visit a site, and some services and functionalities may not work.

To manage cookies in your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Third-party cookies

Some of our partners may set cookies on your device. These third parties have their own independent privacy policies, over which we have no control. For more information, please review the privacy policies of Google, Meta, and Shopify.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Please check this page periodically to stay informed about our use of cookies.
